## 0.1.0

First release for WoW Classic Era.

- **Your bags, sorted:** every item goes in a group (Sell, Keep, Bank or Look at It) with the reason beside it. Quest items and equipment sets are never sold.
- **Selling:** one Sell button at the vendor, 12 items a click, so you can always buy them back. Nothing leaves your bags unless you click.
- **Your lists:** Always Keep, Always Sell and Always Bank, plus Sell Below for anything worth less than an amount you pick.
- **Tooltips and bags:** hover an item to see what Butler says, a free slot count on your backpack button, and a trash can on your cheapest item when your bags are full.
- **For hunters:** one click fills your quiver or ammo pouch with the best ammo you can use.
- **Gold goals:** class spells, profession training and riding, with how close you are, and an arrow to your trainer.
- **Languages:** English, Deutsch (German), French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
